## LinkedIn post 2 — a data lesson (links to the Insights article)

> A raw count can make your most conscientious staff look like your biggest risk.
>
> Someone working four shifts a week will appear in more incident records than a colleague on one. Someone who reports concerns promptly will appear more often than someone who doesn't.
>
> When I built Care Quality Intelligence I used three rules:
> 1. Show a rate next to every count (per 100 delivered sessions).
> 2. Don't rate anyone on a tiny sample.
> 3. Treat good reporting as a strength, not a risk.
>
> You don't need special software to do this — a spreadsheet or Power BI report can apply the same rules.
>
> Short write-up: https://jomovate.com/insights/fair-staff-metrics-in-care/

## Case-study summary 1 — GarageProof UK

**GarageProof UK: evidence-based repair approvals for independent garages** *(Pilot)*

Repair approvals often happen by phone, so when a customer disputes a charge there is little record of what was found, quoted and agreed. GarageProof gives independent garages a digital job flow: check-in, inspection notes with photos, itemised estimates with VAT, and a secure link that lets the customer approve or decline without creating an account. Every decision is timestamped in an audit trail.

Built with React, TypeScript, Express, Prisma and Supabase authentication. Currently in pilot with invited garages; no usage figures are published.

https://jomovate.com/work/garageproof-uk/

## Case-study summary 2 — Performance Insight Explorer

**Performance Insight Explorer: from an unfamiliar spreadsheet to a management report** *(Prototype, live demo)*

Operational data often arrives with unknown columns, gaps and errors. Performance Insight Explorer profiles an uploaded dataset, checks data quality across ten dimensions, lets you define KPIs without code, and produces trend, control-chart and driver analysis with exports to PowerPoint, PDF and Excel.

Built in Python with Streamlit, pandas, statsmodels and Plotly, with an automated test suite. The free-hosted demo can take a minute to wake up.

https://jomovate.com/work/performance-insight-explorer/
